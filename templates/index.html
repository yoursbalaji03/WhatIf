  • PPF & FD: Safe, guaranteed returns — ideal for capital preservation.
  • Gold: Hedge against inflation with moderate returns.
  • SIP: Diversified mutual fund investing — good balance of risk and growth.

  • NPS: Government pension scheme with tax benefits.
